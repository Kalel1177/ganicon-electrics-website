About Ganicon

One company. Multiple technical disciplines. One controlled operating philosophy.

Ganicon Electrics is being structured as a technology and engineering company capable of taking an idea from concept and requirements through design, verification, deployment and controlled improvement.

Rather than treating quality management as an administrative layer, Ganicon is building its Integrated Management System into the way its products, software, records, suppliers and projects are managed from the beginning.
